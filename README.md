## Project Overview
This project explores the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist to evaluate e-commerce performance across product categories, customers, sellers, sales, and delivery operations.

## Business Questions
The analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:
1) What is the overall business performance in terms of total sales, customers, sellers, unique products, and product categories?
2) Which product categories generate the highest Average Order Value (AOV)?
3) Do categories with higher AOV also perform well in terms of total sales and order volume?
4) How do sales and order rankings differ across product categories?
5) What is the relationship between average delivery time and customer review scores?
6) How are delivery statuses distributed across different payment methods?
7)How are sellers geographically distributed across Brazilian states?

## Dashboard Components

The Tableau dashboard includes the following analytical views:

KPI Summary
Provides an overview of overall e-commerce performance.

Butterfly Chart
Compares category AOV against sales and order rankings.

Shipping Days vs Review Score
Examines delivery performance in relation to customer satisfaction.

Seller Distribution Map
Visualizes the geographical distribution of sellers across Brazilian states.

Delivery Status by Payment Method
Examines the distribution of delivery statuses across payment methods.
